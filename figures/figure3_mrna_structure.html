Figure 3. mRNA secondary structure of the CACNA1A R192Q mutation region (CDS positions 550–610). Arcs represent predicted base pairs; arc height and line weight scale with base-pair probability from the RNAfold partition function (Vienna RNA package, Lorenz et al. 2011). Colored baseline dots: per-position accessibility (warm = low accessibility / high pairing probability; cool = high accessibility / predominantly single-stranded). Colored horizontal bars below the backbone indicate the binding windows of the top 10 ranked ASO candidates; teal bars correspond to top-ranked candidates (ranks 1–5, ASR < −1.5 kcal/mol), gray bars to candidates 6–10. Red star and dashed vertical line mark the R192Q mutation site (CDS position 575). The single-stranded region surrounding position 575 is consistent with high accessibility scores in top-ranked candidates.
